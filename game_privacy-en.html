Privacy (Game)

This privacy policy applies specifically to the video game "Tactilite" and its distribution via the Steam platform.

Transparency Note: This game is an offline single-player application. Eloori Interactive does not operate its own game servers and does not collect any personal data about your gaming behavior. However, data is processed by the distribution platform Steam (Valve Corp.).

1. Data Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) is:

2. Data Processing by the Game

a) Local Savegames

All progress, tactics, savegames, and settings are stored exclusively locally on your computer. We, as developers, have no access to these files.

b) No Telemetry by Us

We have not implemented any tracking code, analytics tools, or "phone home" features in the game's code.

c) Contact via Email

If you contact us via email (e.g., for support), we store your email address and your message for as long as necessary to process the request. Afterward, this data will be deleted.

d) Unity Engine

The game was developed using the Unity Engine. Depending on your system configuration, Unity may independently collect anonymized hardware statistics or crash reports for troubleshooting. For more information, please refer to the privacy policy of Unity Technologies.

e) Steam Friends List & Statistics (In-Game)

The game "Tactilite" offers an optional feature that allows you to see a list of your Steam friends who also own the game, along with selected in-game statistics (e.g., number of cups and leagues won, seasons played, in-game money spent, best placement, and preferred playstyle).

Important: This data is retrieved exclusively via the official Steamworks API from Valve Corporation and displayed directly in your game. This data is not transmitted to Eloori Interactive, and we do not operate our own servers to store it.

For this purpose, the feature processes the following data provided by Steam:

  • Steam IDs and public display names (Persona names) of your Steam friends.
  • The game statistics of these friends stored for Tactilite (provided their Steam privacy settings allow this).

Purpose: Displaying the friends list and comparing game progress within the game.

Legal Basis / Consent: The feature is optional. Upon opening the game for the first time, you will be informed about this data processing and can decide for yourself whether the friends' data should be loaded. Processing is based exclusively on your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR; Art. 6 (6) FADP), which you can revoke at any time with effect for the future by no longer using the feature.

Recipients & Data Transfer: The data is provided by Valve Corporation via Steam. Data may be transferred to the USA. Valve's privacy policy applies.

Storage Duration: The data is only processed in the RAM during runtime and is not permanently stored by the game.

3. Data Processing by Steam (Valve Corp.)

The game is distributed and launched via the Steam platform, owned by Valve Corporation. The use of Steam is subject to Valve's privacy policy. Please note that Valve collects data to enable the use of the game.

a) What data does Steam collect?

When you launch the game via Steam, Valve typically processes:

  • Your Account ID and purchase information (Proof of ownership / DRM)
  • Playtime and "last played" status
  • Achievements
  • Technical crash reports (Crash Dumps), if automatically sent to Steam.

b) Transfer to Third Countries (USA)

Valve Corporation is based in the USA (Valve Corporation, P.O. Box 1688, Bellevue, WA 98009, USA). By using Steam, your data will be transferred to the USA. Under data protection law, the USA is considered a third country.

We, as developers, have no influence over data processing by Valve. We only receive anonymized, aggregated statistics from Steam (e.g., "100 sales in Germany", "Average playtime: 2 hours") which do not allow any conclusions to be drawn about individuals.

Further information can be found in the Privacy Policy of Valve.

4. Legal Basis

To the extent that we, as developers, have access to (anonymized) data via Steam at all, processing occurs based on:

  • Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (Performance of a Contract): The provision of the game via Steam is part of the purchase contract.
  • Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest): We use anonymous sales statistics to measure the game's success and fix bugs.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not store personal data ourselves, your claims for information, correction, or deletion are primarily directed at Valve Corporation (Steam), as your user data is stored there.

As far as it concerns us (e.g., email contact), you have the right to request information and deletion at any time. Please contact us at the address provided above.

Additionally, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g., the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) in Switzerland or the competent data protection authority in your country of residence) if you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law.
