Tactics Handbook: The 12 RPG Classes

Behind the Scenes: How the Game Ticks

How is the game world created? (Generation)

Your career starts at the very bottom. The game world consists of three dynamic leagues with a fiercely balanced difficulty curve:

  • League 3 (Strength ~40): Amateur and semi-pro football. You start with €500k and a team full of talents who want to move up.
  • League 2 (Strength ~50): Tough professional football. The transition is doable but requires clever transfers.
  • League 1 (Strength ~65): The international metropolises. Here the true masters play, and the AI will give you nothing for free.

Anti-Trademark Generator: The engine generates dynamic names from real world cities (e.g., Milan, London) and club prefixes. A filter prevents protected names of real world-class clubs from appearing – you play in a completely unique but authentic universe!

Skill Trees, Passives & 12 Player Classes

The level-up system has been completely overhauled. Every player now features an individual Skill Tree with 3 paths defining their class:

  • Randomized Trees: Each tree starts with a central node (+5 Strength). From there, three branches extend outward (+3 Strength each). The path with the most points determines the player's class (e.g., 'Poacher' or 'Playmaker').
  • Passive Effects: On the path to specialization, you unlock powerful bonuses. From 'Iron Lungs' (less fitness loss) and 'Miracle Healing' (shorter injuries) to 'Leader' (form boost for the team).
  • Smart Auto-Level & Cap: Use 'Fast Distribute' to let the AI automatically level the player in their primary role. Once a player reaches their individual potential limit, the rest of the tree locks permanently.
Team Chemistry: The Bond System

Loyalty pays off. Players who spend a long time on the pitch together develop strong team chemistry, represented as Bonds:

  • Four Levels of Loyalty: After 18 matches together, a player reaches 'Silver', after 54 matches 'Gold', and absolute club legends achieve 'Red' status after 10 seasons.
  • Form Boosts & Glowing UI: The higher the bond, the stronger the form bonus (increasing the form cap up to 20 for 'Red' bonds). In the menu, these players are highlighted with elegant, glowing accents.
  • Transfer Market Dynamics: New players start at 'Bronze' (0 bonus). Only the 'Youth Guru' archetype has a chance to scout talents who instantly start with a Silver bond.
Smart Event Tutorial

We hate boring, rigid tutorials. That's why the game uses an intelligent, context-sensitive system:

  • Learn by Playing: The game explains mechanics exactly when they become relevant. Win your first major title, and your Assistant Manager will explain what to do with your new Manager Points.
  • Dynamic Triggers: Deep mechanics like the Bond System or Hardcore Modifiers only pop up when you activate them through your gameplay for the very first time.
The Match Engine, Synergies & The Drama Factor

Matches are simulated in real-time and require strategic thinking when setting up your lineup:

  • Tactical Synergies (Mandatory!): An attack made entirely of 'Poachers' will hardly score any goals (-30% penalty) if 'Playmakers' or 'Supporters' are missing in midfield to deliver the passes. You must combine the right RPG classes to earn bonuses of up to +15% on team sectors.
  • Sector Duels: Your attack fights against the opposing defense, your midfield wrestles for possession. If you play defensively, your defense becomes a bulwark, but hardly anything works upfront.
  • The Drama Factor (Anti-Draw): Nothing is more boring than five 0:0s in a row. Our engine detects when two defensive top teams clash. In 40% of threatening draws, there is a "Lucky Punch" in the 90th minute that decides the game through a counter-attack!
Transfer Market, Relative Rarity & Poaching

The transfer market grows with you. Player rarities (like World Class or Legend) are not rigidly defined but scale relative to the average of your team! A 65-rated player is an absolute legend find in League 3, but absolute standard in League 1.

The Mercenary Threat: The best 5 players on the market have a 30% chance to be "Mercenaries". They elevate your team to the next level but ruin your salary budget. A constant balancing of risk and reward.

Direct Poaching: Missing money on the market? If you desperately want a player from the opposing AI, you can buy him straight out of the enemy squad. This costs you 3 times the market value (only 2 times with a manager skill) – but in crucial phases of the season, it's worth it!
