🔥 Modular Modifier System & Kick-Out Challenge

Forget linear difficulty. Once you've proven you can win, you take fate into your own hands — tune four modular parameters and earn the ultimate "God Manager" achievements.

💰 Financial Difficulty (Lvl 1–5): radically reduced income, exploding transfer prices, salaries that eat your budget.
🧠 XP Reduction (Lvl 1–5): harder progression — players learn up to 25% slower at Level 5.
🚑 Injury Risk (Lvl 1–5): exponential injury risk (2^Level) — up to 32% higher chance at Level 5.
⚔️ The Kick-Out Challenge: the ultimate hardcore mode. Fulfill brutal board challenges or be fired immediately. Game over.
Behind the scenes

How the game ticks

The deep systems under the hood — open any panel to dig in.

How is the world generated?+

Your career starts at the bottom. The world is three dynamic leagues with a tightly balanced difficulty curve:

  • League 3 (Strength ~40): Swiss amateur and pro football. You start with 500k € and a team of hungry talents.
  • League 2 (Strength ~50): tough German pro football. Doable, but it demands clever transfers.
  • League 1 (Strength ~65): international metropolises. The true masters play here, and the AI gives nothing for free.

Anti-Trademark Generator: the engine builds dynamic names from real cities (Milan, London) and club prefixes, with a filter that prevents protected real-world names — a unique but authentic universe.

Team Chemistry: The Bond System+

Loyalty pays off. Players who share the pitch develop chemistry shown as Bonds:

  • Four levels of loyalty: "Silver" after 18 matches, "Gold" after 54, and legends turn "Red" after 10 seasons.
  • Form boosts & glowing UI: higher bonds raise the form cap (up to 20 for "Red"), with elegant glowing accents in the menu.
  • Market dynamics: new players start at "Bronze". Only the Youth Visionary can find talents that arrive with a Silver bond.
Smart Event Tutorial+

We hate rigid tutorials. The game uses an intelligent, context-based system:

  • Learn while playing: mechanics are explained the moment they matter. Win your first title and your assistant explains Manager Points.
  • Dynamic triggers: deep mechanics like Bonds or hardcore modifiers only surface once your gameplay activates them.
RPG Skill-Trees & the 12 Classes+

The level-up system makes Tactilite a true RPG. Every player has a unique skill tree starting at a central node.

  • Class definition: where you invest most points sets the class (a striker becomes "Poacher", "Dribbler", or "Supporter").
  • Passive abilities: unlock game-changers — "Iron Lungs" cuts fitness loss, "Miracle Healing" shortens injuries, a "Leader" lifts the team's form after a win.
  • Smart Auto-Level: use "Fast Distribute" and the AI levels the player intelligently in their primary role.
The Match Engine, Synergies & the Drama Factor+

Matches simulate in real time and reward strategic line-ups:

  • Tactical synergies (mandatory): an attack of pure 'Poachers' barely scores (−30%) without 'Playmakers' or 'Supporters' feeding them. Combine classes for bonuses up to +15%.
  • Sector duels: your attack fights their defense, your midfield wrestles for possession. Park the bus and your defense becomes a wall — but you create nothing up front.
  • The Drama Factor (anti-draw): five 0:0 draws is boring. When two defensive teams clash, 40% of looming draws end with a 90th-minute "Lucky Punch" counter.
Strategy Hub & the Live Match System+

The old On/Off sim is history. Welcome to the Tactics Center and interactive live matches.

  • Live-Match HUD: follow the game live, watch your XI's energy, react to red cards (real suspensions), and switch mentalities on the fly.
  • Data-driven assistant: formation changes (4-3-3, 3-5-2), xG, age structure and average strength visualized via dynamic graphs.
  • Form through performance: strikers and midfielders draw form directly from goal contributions. Score, and you catch fire.
Transfer Market, Contracts & Promises+

Players no longer move for money alone. The market (now 30 players) demands negotiation. Lure stars with contract promises to boost your dice rolls (now 2× 8-sided dice).

  • The 4 promises: a starting-XI guarantee, goal bonuses, a promotion pledge, or the cup-win guarantee.
  • The price of a broken word: every promise triggers a selling lock. Break one at season's end and your finances bleed through penalty fees.
  • Dynamic AI: buy their players and they instantly generate replacements; at season's end they analyze weaknesses and reinforce.
